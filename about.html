Our Story

Five Years of Finding the Right Property for the Right People

ORION was founded in Kovilambakkam, Chennai, with a single conviction: that property buyers deserve honest, informed guidance — not a hard sell. From day one, we operated differently. No cold calls. No inflated commissions. No steering clients toward listings that served us.

What started as a local consultancy serving families in South Chennai has grown into a trusted name across the city. Our clients return. They refer their friends, their colleagues, their parents. That is the only metric we track.

Over five years, we have helped more than two hundred families and investors find, negotiate, and close on properties that matched their real needs — not just their stated budget.

Why ORION

What Makes Us Different

There are hundreds of brokers in Chennai. Here is why clients keep choosing us over them.

01

Verified Properties Only

Every property we show a client has been physically verified by our team. We confirm legal status, actual dimensions, and builder credibility before adding it to our shortlist. You see fewer options — every one of them is worth your time.

02

No Dual Agency

We represent you — not the seller, not the builder. We never take payments from both sides of a transaction. Our only financial interest is in helping you close at a fair price, so that you refer us to your network.

03

End-to-End Handling

Search, negotiation, legal due diligence, documentation, and registration — we manage the full process. You do not need to coordinate between a broker, a lawyer, and a documentation service. One point of contact, start to finish.

04

Local Depth, Broad Reach

Our roots are in Kovilambakkam. Our reach covers all of South Chennai — from Medavakkam to Sholinganallur to Perumbakkam. We know the neighbourhoods, the schools, the infrastructure pipeline, and the price trajectories.
